KMCT COLLEGE OF ENGINEERING

KMCT College of Engineering is a well-known self-financing technical institution established in 2001 by the Komath Mehboob Charitable Trust (KMCT). Located in Kallanthode near NIT Calicut in Kozhikode, Kerala, the college is approved by the AICTE and is affiliated with the APJ Abdul Kalam Technological University (KTU).
